# Manuscript coverage

This table maps the [arXiv v3 manuscript](2-proofs.tex) ([PDF](2-proofs.pdf)) to 25 selected Lean conclusions: 16 outside Section 4, and its eight named results plus the H⁰ remark. [verification/coverage.json](../verification/coverage.json) is the machine-readable inventory. Counts identify claims; they do not measure proof effort or the proportion of every proof sentence translated.

**Corollary 1.3 (`cor:unirational`) is excluded. Lemma 2.3 is included over perfect fields.** Section 2 already states that its ground field is perfect. New Remark 1.5 on del Pezzo surfaces and quartic comparisons is not included among the 25 selected conclusions. The former bibliographic Remark 2.5 is commented out in v3. The required characteristic-zero Coray interface is internal; the entire 1976 Coray paper, including its other field and singular-surface assertions, is not claimed.

Each link below points to a full named provider and its source declaration. A rational section means a morphism Spec(k) → X whose composite with X → Spec(k) is the identity. Hypotheses involving the original subscheme, its embedding, smoothness, length, or geometric position encode the corresponding manuscript data. They are not omitted from the claim merely because the final arithmetic criterion has no external theorem premise.
